## like (+ verb + -ing)
$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|}
\hline
\text{$:blush: :blush:$ }\text{I}\textbf{ love} & \textbf{shopping.} \\
\text{:blush: }\text{I}\textbf{ like} & \textbf{going}\text{ to the cinema.} \\
\text{:confused: }\text{I}\textbf{ don't like} & \textbf{doing}\text{ housework.} \\
\text{:confused: :confused: }\text{I}\textbf{ hate} & \textbf{driveing}\text{ at night.} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

### spelling rules for the *-ing* form
$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Infinitive} & \textbf{Verb}\text{ +}\textit{ -ing} & \textbf{Spelling} \\
\hline
\text{cook} & \text{I like cook}\textbf{ing.} & \text{add -ing} \\
\text{study} & \text{She hates study}\textbf{ing.} & \\
\hline
\text{dance} & \text{I love danc}\textbf{ing.} & \text{- e + add -ing} \\
\hline
\text{shop} & \text{I don't like shop}\textbf{ping.} & \text{one vowel + one consonant = double consonant + -ing} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$
