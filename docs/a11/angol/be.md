# To be (létige)

## Present tense verb *be* + (Jelen idejű ige)

$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|}
\hline
\text{Full form (Teljes alak)} & \text{Contraction (Összevont alak)} \\
\hline
\text{I}\textbf{ am}\text{ your teacher} & \text{I}\textbf{'m}\text{ your teacher} \\
\text{You}\textbf{ are}\text{ in room 13.} & \text{You}\textbf{'re}\text{ in room 13.} \\
\text{He}\textbf{ is}\text{ James.} & \text{He}\textbf{'s}\text{ James.} \\
\text{She}\textbf{ is}\text{ Maria.} & \text{She}\textbf{'s}\text{ Maria.} \\
\text{It}\textbf{ is}\text{ a school.} & \text{It}\textbf{'s}\text{ a school.} \\
\text{We}\textbf{ are}\text{ students.} & \text{We}\textbf{'re}\text{ students.} \\
\text{You}\textbf{ are}\text{ in Class 2.} & \text{You}\textbf{'re}\text{ in Class 2.} \\
\text{They}\textbf{ are}\text{ teachers.} & \text{They}\textbf{'re}\text{ teachers.} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

## Present tense verb *be* - and ?
$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
Full form & Contraction & \\
\hline
\text{I}\textbf{ am not} & \text{I}\textbf{'m not} & \\
\text{You}\textbf{ are not} & \text{You}\textbf{aren't} & \text{Italian} \\
\text{He / She / It}\textbf{ is not} & \text{He / She / It}\textbf{isn't} & \text{Spanish.} \\
\text{We}\textbf{ are not} & \text{We}\textbf{ aren't} & \text{ British.} \\
\text{We}\textbf{ are not} & \text{You}\textbf{ aren't} & \\
\text{They}\textbf{ are not} & \text{They}\textbf{ aren't} & \\
\end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\fbox{?} & & \fbox{$\checkmark$} & & \fbox{$\times$} & \\
\hline
\textbf{Am}\text{ I} & & & \text{I}\textbf{ am.} & & \text{I}\textbf{'m not.} \\
\textbf{Are}\text{ you} & \text{ German?} & & \text{ you}\textbf{ are.} & & \text{ you}\text{ aren't.} \\
\textbf{Is}\text{ he / she / it} & \text{Russian?} & \text{Yes,} & \text{he / she / it}\textbf{ is.} & \text{No,} & \text{he / she / it}\textbf{isn't.} \\
\end{array}
$$
