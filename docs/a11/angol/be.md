# To be (létige)

## Present tense verb *be* + (Jelen idejű létige)

$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Full form (Teljes alak)} & \textbf{Contraction (Összevont alak)} \\
\hline
\text{I}\textbf{ am}\text{ your teacher} & \text{I}\textbf{'m}\text{ your teacher} \\
\text{You}\textbf{ are}\text{ in room 13.} & \text{You}\textbf{'re}\text{ in room 13.} \\
\text{He}\textbf{ is}\text{ James.} & \text{He}\textbf{'s}\text{ James.} \\
\text{She}\textbf{ is}\text{ Maria.} & \text{She}\textbf{'s}\text{ Maria.} \\
\text{It}\textbf{ is}\text{ a school.} & \text{It}\textbf{'s}\text{ a school.} \\
\text{We}\textbf{ are}\text{ students.} & \text{We}\textbf{'re}\text{ students.} \\
\text{You}\textbf{ are}\text{ in Class 2.} & \text{You}\textbf{'re}\text{ in Class 2.} \\
\text{They}\textbf{ are}\text{ teachers.} & \text{They}\textbf{'re}\text{ teachers.} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

## Present tense verb *be* - and ? (Jelen idejű létige - tagadás és kérdés)
$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
Full form & Contraction & \\
\hline
\text{I}\textbf{ am not} & \text{I}\textbf{'m not} & \\
\text{You}\textbf{ are not} & \text{You}\textbf{ aren't} & \text{Italian} \\
\text{He / She / It}\textbf{ is not} & \text{He / She / It}\textbf{isn't} & \text{Spanish.} \\
\text{We}\textbf{ are not} & \text{We}\textbf{ aren't} & \text{ British.} \\
\text{We}\textbf{ are not} & \text{You}\textbf{ aren't} & \\
\text{They}\textbf{ are not} & \text{They}\textbf{ aren't} & \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
\fbox{?} & & \fbox{$\checkmark$} & & \fbox{$\times$} & \\
\hline
\textbf{Am}\text{ I} & & & \text{I}\textbf{ am.} & & \text{I}\textbf{'m not.} \\
\textbf{Are}\text{ you} & \text{ German?} & & \text{ you}\textbf{ are.} & & \text{ you}\text{ aren't.} \\
\textbf{Is}\text{ he / she / it} & \text{Russian?} & \text{Yes,} & \text{he / she / it}\textbf{ is.} & \text{No,} & \text{he / she / it}\textbf{ isn't.} \\
\textbf{Are}\text{ we} & \text{Polish?} & & \text{We}\textbf{ are.} & & \text{we}\textbf{ aren't.} \\
\textbf{Are}\text{ you} & & & \text{you}\textbf{ are.} & & \text{you}\textbf{ aren't.} \\
\textbf{Are}\text{ they} & & & \text{they}\textbf{ are.} & & \text{they}\textbf{ aren't.} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

## Possessive adjectives: *my, your, etc.* (Birtokos névmások: enyém, tiéd, stb.)
$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{I}\text{'m Italian.} & \textbf{My}\text{ family are from Rome.} \\
\textbf{You}\text{'re in level 1.} & \text{This is}\textbf{ your }\text{classroom.} \\
\textbf{He}\text{'s the director.} & \textbf{His}\text{ name is Michael.} \\
\textbf{She}\text{'s your teacher.} & \textbf{Her}\text{ name is Tina.} \\
\textbf{It}\text{'s a school.} & \textbf{Its}\text{ name is Queen's School.} \\
\textbf{We}\text{'re an international school} & \textbf{Our}\text{ students are from many different countries.} \\
\textbf{They}\text{'re new students.} & \textbf{Their}\text{ names are David and Emma.} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

## Articles: *a / an, the*, plurals, *this / that / these / those* (Névelők: egyes és többes szám)
$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|}
\hline
& \textbf{a}\text{ bag.} \\
& \text{  pen.} \\
\hline
\text{It's} & \\
\hline
& \textbf{an}\text{ identity card.} \\
& \text{  umbrella.} 
\hline
\end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
\text{Open} & & \text{door} \\
& \textbf{the} & \\
\text{Close} & & \text{windows.} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

### Plurals (többesszám)
$$
\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Singular} & \textbf{Pulral} & \textbf{Spelling} \\
\hline
\text{a book} & \text{book}\textbf{s} & \text{add -s} \\
\text{a key} & \text{key}\textbf{s} & \\
\hline
\text{a watch} & \text{watch}\textbf{es} & \text{add -es after}\textit{ ch, sh, s, x} \\
\text{a box} & \text{boxes} & \\
\hline
\text{a country} & \text{countr}\textbf{ies} & \text{consonant + y > }\textit{ies} \\
\text{a dictionary} & \text{dictionar}\textbf{ies} & \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

### this / that / these / those
$$
\begin{array}{|l|}
\hline
\text{What's}\textbf{ this}\text{? It's a lighter.} \\
\textbf{That}\text{ car is Italian.} \\
\textbf{These}\text{ watches are Japanese.} \\
\text{What are}\textbf{ those}\text{? They're tables.} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$
