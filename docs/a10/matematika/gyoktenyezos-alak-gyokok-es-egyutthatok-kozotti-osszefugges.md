# A gyöktényezős alak. Gyökök és együtthatók közötti összefüggés
## 2168. feladat
### a. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2} + x - 6 \\[2em]
a &= 1 \\
b &= 1 \\
c &= -6 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1^{2} - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot (-6)}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{25}}{2} = \frac{-1 \pm 5}{2} = \\
x_{1} &= \frac{4}{2} = 2 \\
x_{2} &= - \frac{6}{2} = -3
\end{aligned}
$$

### b. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2} + 7x + 12 \\[2em]
a &= 1 \\
b &= 7 \\
b &= 12 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{7^{2} - 4 \cdot 12}}{2} = \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{49-48}}{2} = \frac{-7 \pm 1}{2} = \\
x_{1} &= - \frac{6}{2} = -3 \\
x_{2} &= - \frac{8}{2} = -4
\end{aligned}
$$

### c. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2} + 2x -35 \\[2em]
a &= 1 \\
b &= 2 \\
c &= -35 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{144}}{2} = \\
x_{1} &= \frac{10}{2} = 5 \\
x_{2} &= - \frac{14}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

### d. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2} + 16x + 60 \\[2em]
a &= 1 \\
b &= 16 \\
c &= 60 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-16 \pm \sqrt{16^{2} - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 60}}{2} = \frac{-16 \pm \sqrt{16}}{2} = \\
x_{1} &= - \frac{12}{2} = -6 \\
x_{2} &= - \frac{20}{2} = -10
\end{aligned}
$$

### e. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& x^{2} - 16x + 64 \\[2em]
a &= 1 \\
b &= -16 \\
c &= 64 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{16 \pm \sqrt{(-16)^{2} - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot \cdot 64}}{2} = \frac{16 \pm \sqrt{256 - 256}}{2} = \\
x_{1} &= \frac{16}{2} = 8 \\
x_{2} &= \frac{16}{2} = 8
\end{aligned}
$$

### f. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 2x^{2} + 7x + 17 \\[2em]
a &= 2 \\
b &= 7 \\
c &= 17 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{7^{2}} - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot 17}{2 \cdot 2} = \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{49 - 136}}{4} = \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{-87}}{4} \\
& \text{A diszkrimináns negatv (-87), így nincs valós gyök}
\end{aligned}
$$

### g. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 2x^{2} - 3x - 9 \\[2em]
a &= 2 \\
b &= -3 \\
c &= -9 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{(-3)^{2} - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot (-9)}}{2 \cdot 2} = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{9 - (-72)}}{4} = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{81}}{4} = \\
x_{1} &= \frac{12}{4} = 3 \\
x_{2} &= - \frac{6}{4} = - \frac{3}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

### h. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 2x^{2} + 28x +98 \\[2em]
a &= 2 \\
b &= 28 \\
c &= 98 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-28 \pm \sqrt{28^{2} - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot 98}}{2 \cdot 2} = \frac{-28 \pm \sqrt{784 - 784}}{4} = - \frac{28}{4} = -7
\end{aligned}
$$

### i. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 3x^{2} + 8x + 4 \\[2em]
a &= 3 \\
b &= 8 \\
c &= 4 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{8^{2} - 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 4}}{2 \cdot 3} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{64 - 48}}{6} = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{16}}{6} = \\
x_{1} &= - \frac{4}{6} = - \frac{2}{3} \\
x_{2} &= - \frac{12}{6} = -2
\end{aligned}
$$

### j. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 6x^{2} + 7x -5 \\[2em]
a &= 6 \\
b &= 7 \\
c &= -5 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{7^{2} - 4 \cdot 6 \cdot (-5)}}{2 \cdot 6} = \frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{169}}{12} = \\
x_{1} &= \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2} \\
x_{2} &= - \frac{20}{12} = - \frac{5}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

### k. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& -2x^{2} - 9x + 18 \\[2em]
a &= -2 \\
b &= -9 \\
c &= 18 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{9 \pm \sqrt{(-9)^{2} - 4 \cdot (-2) \cdot 18}}{2 \cdot (-2)} = \frac{9 \pm \sqrt{81 + 144}}{-4} = \frac{9 \pm \sqrt{225}}{-4} = \\
x_{1} &= - \frac{24}{-4} = 6 \\
x_{2} &= \frac{-6}{-4} = \frac{3}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

### l. feladat
$$
\begin{aligned}
& -12x^{2} + 13x + 4 \\[2em]
a &= -12 \\
b &= 13 \\
c &= 4 \\[2em]
x_{1,2} &= \frac{-13 \pm \sqrt{13^{2} - 4 \cdot (-12) \cdot 4}}{2 \cdot (-12)} = \frac{-13 \pm \sqrt{169 + 192}}{-24} = \frac{-13 \pm \sqrt{361}}{-24} = \\
x_{1} &= \frac{6}{-24} = - \frac{1}{4} \\
x_{2} &= \frac{-32}{-24} = \frac{4}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$

### c. feladat
$$

### e. feladat

$$

$$
